USE LOCALHELP SAFELY

Terms & safety principles

LocalHelp helps people discover and request connections. It does not guarantee a provider, store, profile, price, review, or outcome.

Safety rules

Never send money, passwords, one-time codes, or government documents to another user. Keep communication on-platform, meet in public, verify important details independently, and report suspicious behaviour.

Prohibited activity

Impersonation, fraud, harassment, threats, scams, spam, discrimination, unsafe services, misleading credentials, and attempts to move users to unsafe payment channels are prohibited.

Reports and blocks

Use reporting and blocking tools. Reports may be reviewed by authorized moderators. Do not include identity documents or sensitive personal information in a report.

18+ features

Dating, friendship, and matrimony features are disabled by default until age verification, moderation, abuse escalation, rate limits, and safety operations are ready. Any future age or identity signal will be shown only as a limited status, never as a document or number.

Prototype notice

This is a development prototype. Paid plans, authentication, payments, verification, and service availability are not active until the production providers and legal documents are configured.
