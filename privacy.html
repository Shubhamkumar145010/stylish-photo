PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Privacy at LocalHelp

This plain-language summary explains what the MVP is designed to collect and what it will never collect.

What we may collect

Account contact details, display name, coarse city or area, optional profile photo, service information, order requests, messages, reports, and verification outcomes needed for a feature.

What we never collect on our servers

Government identity images or numbers, Aadhaar, PAN, passport, driving licence, biometric data, or raw identity-provider payloads. If an external provider performs verification, LocalHelp receives only the minimum status and a random provider reference.

How we protect data

Access is authorized server-side. Private API responses are not cached. Reports and moderation actions are restricted. Sensitive data is excluded from logs, analytics, backups, and support exports.

Your choices

You can avoid adding an optional photo, stop using a connection, block or report another user, and request account deletion. Production deletion and retention schedules will be published before launch.

Before launch

This prototype is not a live service. Final legal notices, data-retention periods, processors, contact details, and regional rights will be reviewed and published before collecting real user data.
