PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Privacy at Stylish Name

This plain-language summary explains what the MVP is designed to collect and what it will never collect.

What we may collect

Stylish Name works without an account. The text you enter is processed in your browser to create previews and is not sent to a server by this prototype.

What we never collect on our servers

We do not ask for names linked to identity documents, passwords, government IDs, contacts or payment information. Your text stays in your browser unless you choose to copy or download it.

How we protect data

The generator is designed to be client-side and does not require sign-in. Clear your browser data if you want to remove locally stored preferences or downloads.

Your choices

You can use the generator anonymously, clear your text at any time, and choose whether to copy or download a result.

Before launch

This prototype is not a live service. Final legal notices, data-retention periods, processors, contact details, and regional rights will be reviewed and published before collecting real user data.
